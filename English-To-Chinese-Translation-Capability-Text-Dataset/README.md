# English-to-Chinese Translation Capability (Text) Dataset

This dataset is designed for English-to-Chinese translation, providing paired source English texts and corresponding Chinese translations. It covers common short-text scenarios such as explanations, as well as sentence- and paragraph-level samples, with an emphasis on semantic consistency and preserving the original tone and style where possible.
The dataset keeps translation behavior consistent for named entities and domain-specific terminology (and can be further aligned with a project-specific glossary or preferred term mapping). It is suitable for training, evaluating, and fine-tuning AI large models for translation alignment, including multi-modal alignment workflows.
It can also serve as a text-semantic bridge for world-model and 3D asset generation pipelines, improving cross-lingual understanding and reliable semantic mapping between English and Chinese.
